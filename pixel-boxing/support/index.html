How to Play

  • JAB / CROSS — tap to punch.
  • GUARD — hold to block incoming punches.
  • Directional arrows — duck left/right or step back to avoid attacks.
  • Duck and punch on the same side at the same time to land a body shot.

A Practice Room with an in-app tutorial is available from the main menu to try out all controls before a real match.

Game Center

Game Center is optional and only used for the Total Wins leaderboard. If you are not signed in to Game Center, the game continues to work normally offline.
