RIVAL — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Summary

RIVAL is designed to work fully offline. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information about you.

Data We Do Not Collect

RIVAL does not require account creation or login, and does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any user-generated content. There is no analytics or advertising SDK included in the app.

Game Center

RIVAL includes an optional Game Center leaderboard (Total Wins). If you choose to sign in to Game Center, Apple manages your Game Center player identity according to Apple's own privacy policy. RIVAL itself does not store or access your Game Center identity outside of submitting your win count to the leaderboard.

Children's Privacy

RIVAL does not knowingly collect any personal information from children, as it does not collect personal information from any user.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in the future, the updated version will be posted on this page.
